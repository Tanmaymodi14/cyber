Access Control Policy

1. Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for access control within our organization.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors.

3. Responsibilities
- IT Department: Implement access controls
- HR Department: Manage user provisioning
- Security Team: Monitor access violations

4. Access Control Requirements
- Users must authenticate before accessing systems
- Access must be granted based on job function
- Regular access reviews must be conducted
- Unused accounts must be disabled

5. Compliance
This policy ensures compliance with AC-1 requirements.
