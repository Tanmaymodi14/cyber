Access Control Policy and Procedures
Purpose: Define access control policy and associated procedures.
Scope: Organization-wide.
Roles and Responsibilities: CISO, System Owners.
Management Commitment: The organization commits to implementing access control.
Coordination: Coordination among organizational entities.
Compliance: Compliance with FedRAMP.
Procedures: Steps to implement access control.
Review Cadence: Policy reviewed every 3 years; procedures annually.
