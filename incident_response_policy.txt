INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes procedures for incident response training and security awareness within our organization.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel who have access to organizational systems.

3. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
All personnel must complete incident response training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter.

4. TRAINING COMPONENTS
- Security incident identification and classification
- Escalation procedures and contact information
- Tabletop exercises simulating real incidents
- Phishing awareness and response procedures
- Data breach notification requirements

5. TABLETOP EXERCISES
The organization will conduct tabletop exercises quarterly to test incident response capabilities.

6. AWARENESS PROGRAMS
Regular security awareness communications will be distributed to all staff monthly.

7. TRAINING RECORDS
All training completion records will be maintained for audit purposes.

8. SIMULATION TESTING
Annual simulation testing will validate the effectiveness of training programs.
